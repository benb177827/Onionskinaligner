Help & shortcuts

Pan view: drag to pan · scroll wheel zooms (any mode).

Calibrate: click reference point A, then B, on the background. Enter their global coordinates. Scale + rotation are worked out automatically.

Add points: click on the overlay to pin a point of interest. Points stick to the overlay as you move it.

Move overlay: drag to move · Shift+scroll = scale · Alt+scroll = rotate · arrow keys nudge (Shift = fine).
